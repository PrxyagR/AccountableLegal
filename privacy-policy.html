Accountable is a local-first screen-time accountability app. This policy describes what the Accountable iOS app processes, how it is used, where it is stored, and how it can be deleted.

Information processed on your device

Accountable stores app rules, Family Controls selections, onboarding answers, local profile information, token and reward history, exercise completion records, analytics aggregates, notification preferences, and Intentional Apps session history on the device and its shared App Group when an extension needs the same state.

If you create a local profile, the app stores your display name, email address, password verifier, recovery-key verifier, and account/session metadata locally. Passwords and recovery keys are not stored in plaintext.

Permissions and sensitive data

  • Family Controls / Screen Time: iOS provides opaque tokens for selected apps, categories, and web domains. Accountable does not upload raw Screen Time history to an Accountable server.
  • HealthKit: If granted, Accountable reads daily step data for walking rewards. It does not write HealthKit data, sell it, or use it for advertising.
  • Motion: Motion data can be processed on-device for exercise verification, pedometer fallback, and Focus Lock placement checks.
  • Camera: Camera exercise verification processes frames live on-device for pose estimation. Frames and pose samples are not stored as video or uploaded by Accountable.
  • Notifications: Reminders are scheduled locally; the App Store release does not use remote push tokens.

Permissions are optional and can be changed in iOS Settings. If a permission is declined, the related feature may be unavailable while the rest of the app remains usable.

Web views and third-party services

Intentional Apps and SocialLite may display public web destinations operated by third parties. Those sites may process requests, cookies, login details, and content under their own terms and privacy policies. Accountable does not sell or upload your social passwords or browsing content to an Accountable server.

The App Store release does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, App Tracking Transparency, or an Accountable cloud profile service. StoreKit processes purchases and billing through Apple.

Sharing, retention, and deletion

Accountable does not sell personal information or use health, motion, camera, or Screen Time data for advertising. Local records remain on the device until you remove them, delete the app, or use an available reset/deletion control.

The Profile tab provides account export and Delete account. Deleting an account removes Accountable's local profile, credentials, recovery key, local app data, and session from that device. Apple subscription billing is separate: deleting an Accountable profile does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple Account settings.

You can revoke permissions in iOS Settings and clear website data from the Accountable settings surface. For a privacy request that cannot be completed in the app, use the support page.

Future features

Remote AI review and remote peer approval are disabled in the App Store release configuration. If a future release enables them, this policy, consent screens, retention controls, and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.
