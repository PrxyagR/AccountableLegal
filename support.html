Need help? Use the Accountable support tracker while the publisher’s monitored support inbox is being finalized. Do not include passwords, recovery keys, Screen Time tokens, camera frames, health records, or social-media credentials.

Account controls

For local account deletion, use Profile → Delete account in the app. For an export or privacy request that cannot be completed there, use the support tracker without including sensitive information.

Before App Store submission

The publisher must replace the temporary tracker with a monitored support contact and legal publisher identity. The support page must remain publicly reachable without a GitHub login, and the same public privacy-policy URL must be entered in App Store Connect.
